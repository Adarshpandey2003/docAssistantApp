# Privacy Policy for DocAssistant

**Last updated: 24 September 2026**

DocAssistant is an offline document utility for Android. This policy explains
what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect
any of it.

## Data we collect

**None.**

DocAssistant has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. The app contains no
network code at all — it never transmits your documents, images, signatures or
any other information anywhere. Everything you do in the app happens on your
device and stays there.

## Permissions and why they are requested

**Camera** — used only when you open the scanner, so you can photograph a
document. Photos are written to the app's own storage on your device. They are
not uploaded.

**Photos and media** — used only when you choose to import an image or select a
document to convert or compress. The app reads only the files you explicitly
pick.

## Data stored on your device

Scanned pages, generated PDFs, saved signatures and app settings are stored in
DocAssistant's private storage area on your device. Uninstalling the app removes
this data. Files you explicitly export or share are saved wherever you choose to
put them and are then managed by you, not by DocAssistant.

## Sharing

DocAssistant does not share data with us or with any third party. When you use
the share function, Android hands the file to the app you pick — that app's own
privacy policy then applies to it.

## Third-party services

DocAssistant contains no advertising networks, no tracking SDKs, and no
third-party analytics.

## Children

DocAssistant is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. As it
collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Your rights

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access,
export or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control: delete individual
documents inside the app, or uninstall it to remove everything.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, this policy will
be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
